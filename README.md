
BudgetTracker 📊
Money Made Easy

Take control of your finances with smart budgeting, expense tracking, and powerful financial insights — all in one modern mobile app.

✨ Overview

BudgetTracker is a personal finance management app designed to help users manage their money efficiently. From tracking expenses to setting budgets and analyzing spending habits, the app provides an intuitive and secure experience tailored for everyday financial planning.

🚀 Features
🏦 Account Management
Centralized Dashboard
Get a complete overview of your income, expenses, and savings in one place.
Multiple Wallet Support
Manage finances across different bank accounts and digital wallets.
Secure Authentication
User data is protected using Firebase Authentication and encrypted storage practices.
💸 Expense & Budget Tracking
Quick Expense Entry
Easily add expenses with categories, notes, and amounts.
Receipt Uploads
Store receipt images for better financial record-keeping.
Custom Categories
Create personalized categories for both income and expenses.
Monthly Budget Goals
Set spending limits and monitor your progress throughout the month.
📈 Reports & Analytics
Interactive Charts
Visualize weekly and monthly spending trends.
Financial Insights
Analyze top spending categories and monitor overall financial habits.
Forecast Tracking
View estimated spending predictions based on current activity.
